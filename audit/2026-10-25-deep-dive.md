# Deep dive, 2026-10-25

Scott asked: is there real value in this app for him, his associates and APS; does every message sound like a person talking; are the tips aligned with APS goals and the job; how do we make call listening give real, measurable feedback; and is it accurate, tracking trends, showing what works and where to pivot.

How this was checked: the real call pipeline was run on the one real saved call (Christi, Sep 29) and the two fixture calls; the real backup (`state/latest.json`, read only) was used for usage counts, coaching logs and 41 weeks of team trends; two read-only audits ran over every message generator (17,173 outputs captured from the test run plus a fresh baseline) and every tip. Nothing was changed in the app.

## Bottom line

- **Value is real.** For Scott: the paste-to-Verint-to-email flow, the private round and the reviews save hours and keep the wording consistent. For associates: praise first, specific, goal-tied messages with placings. For APS: verification breaches are caught from the transcript (the red flag box), coaching is documented in Verint, and every message is tied to the 2026 goals.
- **Three gaps stop it being the tool Scott described.**
  1. Call feedback is not measurable yet, and the reader still misreads ordinary calls (8 misreads found on 3 calls, below).
  2. Nothing can say what is working. The main Calls flow logs no coaching; the Today page logs a tip on every draft; the two tip trackers never meet.
  3. Usage is thin: 1 saved call, 7 coaching records, 0 follow-ups in the backup. The measurable parts sit inside the closed More fold.

## 1. Call listening

### What works
- Verification as a sequence (name plus last four, DL or password before any disclosure) is right and was right on the real call.
- The confusion finder caught the real problem on Christi's call: the customer lost the payment arrangement at 5:33 after one long run of numbers. The suggested fix (split past due from this month's bill, ask them to say back the next date and amount) is exactly the coaching.
- Silence and holds are measured from the timestamps; the Verint phrase lists are the real ones; the praise floor means no call comes back with nothing good said.

Grammar slips in the same area: "All 1 disclosures", "used 1 times", a doubled full stop after a quote, a hard-coded "she" in a prompt. The app's own email repeats its opener ("There are things in there that worked" then "Solid call, and there are a few things you should keep doing") and quotes garbled speech to text in brackets, which reads like a machine wrote it.

### What "measurable" should mean: a call scorecard (proposal)
On the main path, straight under the paste, the same lines on every call, from the APS Interaction Review form and the KPIs they move:

1. Branded greeting and name
2. Verified before any account information (name plus last four, DL or password)
3. Empathy, only when the customer had a problem
4. Hold done right (asked, roughly how long, thanked, checked back)
5. No long unexplained silence
6. Explained clearly (no confusion left hanging)
7. The right option offered when it fit (arrangement, budget billing, assistance, autopay or online)
8. Next steps with the when and the amount
9. Recap
10. "Anything else" before the close

Each line is Yes, Missed, Didn't apply, or Check, with the time to listen at. One click corrects a line; the corrected card is what is saved, so the record is true even when the reader is not. The card is saved on the call as data, not text, which is what makes trends possible.

Numbers under the card: call length against the 426 second goal, and where the extra went (holds, quiet, the tail after "you're all set"); holds and the longest; longest silence; Verint scored phrases used and penalised phrases said; confusion moments; talk share on labelled calls. Score: "8 of the 9 that applied".

### Trends from calls (proposal)
- Per associate, last 8 calls: which lines keep missing ("recap missed on 4 of 5"), call length against the goal.
- Team, this month: the lines missed most, by call type ("next steps missed on 6 of 10 payment arrangement calls"). That is the pivot signal for huddles and Oscar refreshers.
- Every line maps to the KPIs it moves (the bridge's EVIDENCE_MAP already does this), so a pattern on calls can be checked against the weekly numbers.

## 2. Is the coaching working? (tracking)

- **The weekly data is there.** 41 full weekly uploads in 2026 (Jan 5 to Oct 18), plus months and quarters. Measurement is not the blocker.
- **The record of what was coached is the blocker.**
  - The main Calls flow (summary, Copy For Verint, Copilot email) logs nothing. Only the metric chips under More log, and only they log which tips were given.
  - Coach page and trend email log the metric, never the tips, with a label like "this period" for the week.
  - The Today page logs a tip every time a message is drafted: 103 of 255 logged tips came within 15 minutes of another for the same person and metric, so redrafts count as tips given.
  - Two separate trackers judge tips two different ways: Center > Trends > Patterns (Today page tips, 3 weeks before and after, no team comparison) and People > Coach (Coach and call chip events, the next week, judged against the team's move). Neither sees the other's data.
- **What it should do:** log when something goes out (copied, sent to Outlook, Copy For Verint), not when it is drafted; one log for every channel; one "What's working" view: per metric and per tip, the move at 1 and 3 weeks judged against the team's move over the same weeks, with the sample size shown and nothing called effective under 5 uses.
- **Stray data:** `userCustomTips.transfers` holds "Test Upload 2". It can be offered to an associate as a tip. Needs Scott's OK to delete (it is in his synced data).

## 3. Message tone

Read naturally: call drafts, call feedback, celebration DMs, high fives, the quick check-in prompt (the model to copy). Partly: Today private round, Monday kickoff, check-ins (same block order every time: praise, bridge line, "Focus: X. N, YTD N, target N.", "Tip:"). Not yet: Rankings emails, quarter recap email, Friday week progress, growth message, midweek check-in, follow-up email, sentiment summary, Verint note.

Worst offenders, all confirmed in code:
- Rankings monthly email is a report ("Average Handle Time: 405s (goal: 426s or lower). Met." five times, then "That is 5 of 5 goals met so far. Happy to walk through any of it.") `center-ranking.module.js` 2721 to 2795. Its month over month and YTD siblings put an emoji on every line and say "better by 60s".
- Focus lines that scold: "This one needs real attention.", "It's been a pattern, not a blip.", "We need to turn this around." `morning-pulse.module.js` FOCUS_PERSISTENT, 282 to 288.
- "Others have been moving ahead of you on this" and "the floor has been passing you here" `year-standing.module.js` 37 to 41, close to the banned "people ahead of you".
- "First, the good news... closest to goal" said about a miss.
- Stacked numbers: "628s last week, YTD at 626s, target 426s." and "(617s to 415s)" in more than half of the morning pulse messages.
- Corporate lines: "We identified Average Handle Time as the one to move this week.", "the lever to pull", "new week, new opportunity".
- Team post: "#1 in the Call Center" and "Tied for 4th best... Top 5!"; a median of 14 exclamation marks a post.
- Copilot prompts that push stiffness: the trend prompt opens "You are the AI Intelligence Layer..." with "LEADERSHIP COMPETENCY CONNECTION"; the weekly coaching prompt asks for "clean bullets" and "numbers exactly as given".
- Repetition: "Happy to walk through any of it." closes all four email types; "better than N of N associates!" 1,672 times in the captured DMs; "There's nothing left to ask for." 394 times in high fives.
- Possible live bug: the trend prompt can describe a 0 second handle time as "fell by 600 seconds ... which is better" (no no-calls check in `metric-trends.module.js` coachableTrendMetrics). To verify.

## 4. Tips

The pool fits the job: 536 tips, tips.csv and the embedded copy identical, no follow-up emails, texting, CRM or knowledge base, rookie framing, callback offers on hold, or em dashes; the Positive and Negative Word tips match the Verint lists closely. What needs fixing:

- **Live stray tip:** "Test Upload 2" in the custom Transfers tips can go out as a tip.
- **52 problem tips**, the worst:
  - Identity: "Verify identity every single time" (an authorized call needs only the name); "Pre-fill customer verification info during greeting"; an Overall Experience tip that opens "instead of jumping straight into verification".
  - Promises an exception: "...so we can give you next steps and keep you in adherence."
  - Hurts another goal: "If you know the answer by their second sentence, go to it." (cuts the customer off; the most used AHT tip on the Today page); "Most customers want the number and the date, not the history lesson." (the reason is what stops the high bill callback); "Give yourself 30 seconds between difficult calls" (ACW and adherence); two Negative Word swaps that steer to a transfer.
  - Overall Sentiment tips quoting words Verint does not score ("Take care", "I'm going to help you"); one calls a scored phrase "robotic".
  - Doubtful facts: "Two minutes late four times hurts your number more than one long absence"; "Log in 2 to 3 minutes early" (possible off the clock work).
  - Hold Time: "...that isn't a hold, tell them what you'll do instead" (the only instead is a callback).
- **Gaps:** Overall Experience has 16 tips, about 6 of them its own; no tips on solar bills, reconnect timing, payment posting time, rate plan start dates or scam calls; Transfers never says when to transfer at once (downed line, sparks); Rep Sat has no verification tip, and the call bridge looks for "date of birth" and "security question", which are not the APS standard; 18 of the 24 either-side Managing Emotions phrases ("ridiculous", "totally unacceptable") have no tip.
- **Selection bugs (confirmed in code):**
  - Tip edits and deletions are applied once when the pool loads and again by position when a tip is read, so deleting one tip removes two.
  - Edits are read from browser storage but saved to the app's database, so each new edit wipes the last.
  - Neither bug has hit yet: there are no edits in the backup.
- **Selection bugs (from the audit, not re-run):**
  - Coach page tips are random with no memory.
  - Calls page tips always return the same top two for a finding (ties broken alphabetically).
  - Metrics are ranked by mixing seconds with percentage points, so AHT, ACW and hold outrank survey goals (151 of 255 Today page tips were AHT).
  - The Today page's no-repeat memory is keyed by exact text, so the Oct 14 rewording orphaned 134 of 139 records.
- **39 near-duplicate clusters.**

Full list with line numbers and rewrites: `audit/2026-10-25-tips-findings.md`. Items marked "unsure" there need Scott's answer (can associates dial out, see their own call recordings, see daily transfer or ACW numbers; does call out mean call or text the supervisor).

## 5. Plan (waiting on Scott's approval)

Order is a suggestion. Each phase is tested first, pushed, and checked on the real data.

- **A. Call reader accuracy (small).** The 8 misreads and the grammar slips above, each pinned by a test built from the real call. Needs: 3 to 5 more real Verint pastes (colour copy) to test against.
- **B. Call scorecard.** The 10 lines, the numbers, one-click corrections, saved as data, on the main path (the More fold keeps the rest).
- **C. Close the loop.** Log on send, one log for every channel, redrafts collapsed; one "What's working" view with the team trend table above and per tip results.
- **D. Call trends.** Per associate and team patterns from saved scorecards, by call type.
- **E. Tone.** Rankings and recap emails, focus lines, year standing pool, stacked numbers, the dashboard style messages, team post placings, the stiff Copilot prompts, and the repetition pools.
- **F. Tips.** Fix the two edit and delete bugs; rewrite or cut the 52 problem tips (both copies of the CSV); fill the gaps (Overall Experience, utility call types, safety transfers, verification, Managing Emotions phrases); merge duplicates; give every channel the same no-repeat memory, keyed by tip id rather than text; rank metrics by distance from goal on a common scale.
